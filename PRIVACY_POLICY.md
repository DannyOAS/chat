## Information We Collect

### 1. Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Information:** Username, email address, password, name
- **Profile Information:** Bio, phone number, timezone, language preferences
- **Billing Information:** Payment method details (processed by Stripe)
- **Knowledge Content:** Documents, files, and text you upload
- **Chat Messages:** Conversations with your AI chatbot
- **Support Communications:** Messages you send to our support team

### 2. Information Collected Automatically

- **Usage Data:** Pages visited, features used, time spent
- **Device Information:** Browser type, operating system, device type
- **Log Data:** IP address, timestamps, request/response data
- **Cookies:** Session cookies, preference cookies, analytics cookies

### 3. Information from Third Parties

- **OAuth Providers:** If you sign in with Google or Microsoft, we receive basic profile information
- **Payment Processors:** Stripe provides payment confirmation and billing details
- **Analytics Services:** Aggregated usage statistics (anonymized)

We do not sell your personal information. We share your information only in the following circumstances:

### 1. Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party providers who help us operate our service:

- **Stripe:** Payment processing
- **OpenAI:** AI language model API
- **Pinecone:** Vector database for knowledge retrieval
- **AWS/DigitalOcean:** Cloud hosting and storage
- **Sentry:** Error tracking and monitoring
- **SendGrid:** Email delivery

These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only for the purposes we specify.

### 2. Legal Compliance

We may disclose your information when required by law or to:

- Comply with subpoenas, court orders, or legal process
- Enforce our Terms of Service
- Protect our rights, property, or safety
- Protect the rights, property, or safety of others
- Prevent fraud or illegal activity

### 3. Business Transfers

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred. We will notify you before your information is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

### 4. With Your Consent

We may share your information with other parties when you give us explicit permission to do so.

## Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our service and comply with legal obligations:

- **Account Data:** Retained while your account is active
- **Chat Messages:** Retained for 2 years or until you request deletion
- **Knowledge Content:** Retained while your account is active or until you delete it
- **Audit Logs:** Retained for 7 years (compliance requirement)
- **Deleted Data:** Purged from backups after 30 days

You can request deletion of your data at any time via the GDPR data deletion endpoint or by contacting privacy@shoshchat.ai.

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

### 1. Right to Access

You can request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.

**How to exercise:** Use the `/api/v1/gdpr/export/` endpoint or contact privacy@shoshchat.ai

### 2. Right to Rectification

You can request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.

**How to exercise:** Update your profile via the dashboard or contact us

### 3. Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten)

You can request deletion of your personal data.

**How to exercise:** Use the `/api/v1/gdpr/delete-account/` endpoint or contact privacy@shoshchat.ai

### 4. Right to Restriction of Processing

You can request that we limit how we use your data.

**How to exercise:** Contact privacy@shoshchat.ai

### 5. Right to Data Portability

You can request your data in a machine-readable format.

**How to exercise:** Use the `/api/v1/gdpr/export/` endpoint

### 6. Right to Object

You can object to our processing of your data for direct marketing.

**How to exercise:** Unsubscribe from emails or contact privacy@shoshchat.ai

### 7. Right to Withdraw Consent

You can withdraw consent for optional data processing at any time.

**How to exercise:** Use the `/api/v1/gdpr/consent/` endpoint or contact us

### 8. Right to Lodge a Complaint

You can file a complaint with a supervisory authority in your jurisdiction.

## Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

### Technical Measures

- **Encryption:** TLS 1.3 for data in transit, database encryption at rest
- **Authentication:** Secure password hashing (bcrypt), 2FA/TOTP support
- **Access Controls:** Role-based permissions, principle of least privilege
- **Monitoring:** Real-time security monitoring and alerting
- **Auditing:** Comprehensive audit logging

### Organizational Measures

- **Security Training:** Regular security awareness training for staff
- **Incident Response:** Documented incident response procedures
- **Vendor Management:** Security reviews of all third-party providers
- **Regular Audits:** Internal and external security audits

### Your Responsibilities

- Use a strong, unique password
- Enable two-factor authentication
- Keep your credentials confidential
- Report suspicious activity immediately
- Keep your software up to date
